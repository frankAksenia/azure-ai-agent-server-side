# Invoice and Billing Policy

## Charges and invoices

Monthly billing is generated on the first day of each calendar month. Customers receive an invoice by email and can also view it in the billing portal.

## Disputed charges

If a customer disputes a charge, they should open a billing support ticket within 14 days of the invoice date. The support team will review the original order, usage history, and payment records before issuing a correction.

## Refunds

Refunds are issued only for unused prepaid time or for billing errors confirmed by the finance team. Subscription cancellations are prorated using the remaining calendar days in the current billing period.

## Plan changes

Customers can upgrade or downgrade plans at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades apply at the start of the next billing cycle unless otherwise confirmed in writing.

## Payment methods

Credit cards, ACH transfers, and invoicing are accepted for eligible accounts. Expired cards may result in a brief service interruption until the new payment method is verified.
