# Account Access and Customer Support

## Password reset

Customers can reset their password from the sign-in page. If the reset email is not received, the customer should verify spam folders and confirm the account email address.

## Account lockouts

Accounts are temporarily locked after multiple failed sign-in attempts. A customer can regain access after a password reset or by contacting support if the account is associated with an enterprise identity provider.

## Contact policy

Support requests should include the customer name, account ID, email address, and a short description of the issue. This helps the team route the case to the correct specialist quickly.

## Response expectations

Standard support requests are answered within 1 business day. Critical production incidents are prioritized based on service impact and customer tier.
