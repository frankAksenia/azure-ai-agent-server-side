# Incident Triage and Troubleshooting

## Before escalation

Check service health, recent deployments, error logs, and whether the problem is limited to one tenant, region, or feature area.

## Impact classification

- Sev 1: complete downtime or major data loss risk
- Sev 2: significant degradation with user impact
- Sev 3: limited issue affecting a subset of customers
- Sev 4: minor issue with a clear workaround

## Suggested troubleshooting flow

1. Confirm the scope of the incident.
2. Review the last deployment or configuration change.
3. Check the status of dependent services.
4. Validate authentication and permissions.
5. Capture logs, traces, and screenshots before escalating.

## Communication

Customer-facing updates should include the nature of the problem, affected systems, current status, and next update time.
