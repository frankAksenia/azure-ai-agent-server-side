# Refunds, Cancellations, and Account Changes

## Refund requests

Refunds are reviewed for unused prepaid service, duplicate charges, or confirmed billing errors. Requests should include the invoice number and account ID.

## Cancellations

Customers may cancel a subscription from the account settings page. Cancellation requests are processed at the next renewal date unless the customer requests an immediate stop.

## Service pause

A temporary pause can be requested when the customer is waiting for internal approval or a seasonal change. Pauses are not guaranteed to be available for every plan type.

## Escalations

Escalation requests should be used when the issue affects a production environment or when the customer has not received a response within the expected service window.
