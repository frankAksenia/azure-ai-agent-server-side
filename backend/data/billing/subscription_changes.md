# Subscription Changes and Credits

## Upgrade process

Customers can upgrade to a higher plan at any time. The remaining balance on the current invoice is applied to the new charge for the current billing cycle.

## Downgrade process

Downgrades are scheduled for the next renewal date. If the service is paused or downgraded early, a prorated credit may be applied depending on the account history.

## Credits

Service credits are usually granted for outage windows lasting more than 30 minutes. Credits are calculated based on the service tier and the exact duration of downtime.

## Cancellation

Cancellation requests must be submitted before the renewal date to avoid an automatic charge. A final invoice is generated after the cancellation request is approved.
